Sources and caveats

  • Token volume: OpenRouter rankings (CC BY 4.0). OpenRouter carries only part of all AI traffic: people who call OpenAI, Anthropic or Google directly, or through enterprise contracts, don't show up. The public chart names the top ~9 models each week and groups the rest (~35–50% of tokens) as "Others".
  • Prices: OpenRouter's public price list (/api/v1/models), historical copies from the Internet Archive, and snapshots saved on every refresh. Each model's price is weighted by the measured ratio of prompt to completion tokens. Free and unpriced stealth models count as $0. "Others" is excluded from price, and its token share is shown as price coverage.
  • Elasticity: the rolling regression slope of weekly changes in log volume on weekly changes in log price. New model releases, new use cases (agents, coding) and price cuts all happen at once, so treat it as a descriptive statistic, not a causal demand estimate.
  • World adoption: Microsoft AI Economy Institute, AI Diffusion Report, via Our World in Data.
  • US business adoption: US Census Bureau, Business Trends and Outlook Survey (every two weeks).
  • Usage frequency: Gallup workforce survey and Pew Research Center (entered by hand in data/manual/intensity.csv).
